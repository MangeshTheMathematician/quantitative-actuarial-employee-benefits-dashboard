EMPLOYEE CENSUS DESIGN
- Raw census rows: 10,000
- Unique employee IDs: 9,950
- Rows participating in duplicate employee IDs: 100
- Controlled issues include missing dates/salaries, duplicate IDs, salary outliers, status/date conflicts, missing plan IDs, invalid joining ages, and selected PF/EDLI inconsistencies.
- Derived actuarial outputs are intentionally excluded from the raw census and are to be created by the Python modelling pipeline.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- One employee master feeds separate Gratuity, Superannuation/Pension, EDLI and GTI engines.
- Plan IDs live on employee records; plan formulas and editable parameters live in plan_rules_assumptions.csv.
- Benefit outputs are recalculated dynamically from plan rules. EDLI remains effective-date/statutory and is not freely user-designed.
- plan_assets.csv contains pooled Gratuity and DB pension plan assets, not individual DC member corpus.
- salary_history.csv contains historical product-relevant salary bases and is intentionally long-format by employee/effective date.
- claims_history.csv combines exposure records (product_type=Experience) with product-specific claim records so decrement experience and claims analysis can be separated correctly.

IMPORTANT
- All data are synthetic and fictional for educational/dashboard development use.
- EDLI values in historical synthetic claim rows are illustrative observed records. Production processing must use the official effective-date rule set/calculator.
